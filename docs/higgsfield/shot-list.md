# FreightFlow — Higgsfield shot list

Five clips, one per chapter of the site. For each: upload the matching JPG as the
**start frame** (image-to-video), paste the prompt, generate.

## Settings for every clip
- Model: Seedance 2.5 (the unlimited one on Ultra)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Resolution: 720p (the highest the unlimited allowance covers)
- Length: 8–10 seconds
- Sound: off
- Generate 3–4 versions of each and keep the best

## What to look for when picking a take
- The "FreightFlow" wordmark on the trailer stays readable and correctly spelled
- The truck stays navy blue with the orange stripe, white trailer
- Motion is steady — no sudden cuts, zooms or camera shake
- Wheels turn, the road moves, nothing warps or melts
